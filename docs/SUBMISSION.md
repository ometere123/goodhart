# Submission proposal

**Title:** GOODHART — consensus-backed proxy/objective divergence gate

**Description (under 1000 characters):**

Goodhart prevents autonomous incentive contracts from paying solely because a
proxy metric improved. A programme permanently freezes its underlying objective,
public outcome criteria, numerical proxy threshold, three HTTPS evidence
surfaces, beneficiary, and assessment window. GenLayer validators independently
re-fetch the sources, verify exact proxy figures, semantically assess two
distinct public outcome reports, and adversarially scrutinize positive findings.
Deterministic logic alone derives REWARDABLE, DIVERGENT, UNSUPPORTED or
INCONCLUSIVE. The immutable receipt and definition fingerprint can be consumed
through `is_rewardable()` by a separate one-shot contract. Includes malicious
leader tests, changed-source tests, fixed-int arithmetic and native GenVM
Direct Mode verification. No frontend, funds custody or unverifiable causal
claims; reports are explicitly bounded to the public evidence supplied.

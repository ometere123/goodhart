# GOODHART controlled deterioration fixture B

This bounded fixture corroborates deterioration rather than proxy success. It is not independent proof of a real-world event, publisher identity, causality, payment eligibility, or user outcome.

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: the same 100 tracked support cases.
Underlying objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Outcome indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

Ticket throughput rose, but the same cohort recorded 10 unresolved defects before the period and 31 during it, while repeat support failures rose from 5 to 18. The public outcome is materially worse despite the proxy increase.

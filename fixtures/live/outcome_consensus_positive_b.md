# Controlled GOODHART measurement report B

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: the same 100 tracked support cases.
Objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Outcome indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

For the same 100 cases, unresolved defects fell from 40 at baseline to 10 in the current period, while repeat support failures fell from 20 to 5. The period and population did not change. This is affirmative evidence that the underlying objective improved, not merely evidence that more tickets were closed.

# Outcome report B

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: 100 tracked support cases.
Objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Outcome indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

The same population and period show unresolved defects falling from 40 to 10 and repeat support failures falling from 20 to 5. Both measured outcomes improved, so this report supports improvement of the underlying objective rather than only an increase in ticket throughput.

# GOODHART controlled outcome fixture A

This is a bounded public fixture for demonstrating GOODHART mechanics. It is not independent proof of a real-world event, publisher identity, causality, payment eligibility, or user outcome.

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: all 100 tracked support cases in this fixture.

Underlying objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Outcome indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

Before the period, the fixture recorded 40 unresolved defects and 20 repeat support failures. During the period, it recorded 10 unresolved defects and 5 repeat support failures. The same declared population and measurement window are used for both comparisons. These observations support an improved underlying objective within this fixture's stated evidence limits.

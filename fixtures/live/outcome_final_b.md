# GOODHART demonstration outcome B

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: 100 tracked support cases.
Objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

The same population had 40 unresolved defects and 20 repeat support failures before the period, and 10 unresolved defects and 5 repeat support failures during the period. This is affirmative evidence that the underlying objective improved, not merely evidence that more tickets were closed.

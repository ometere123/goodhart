# GOODHART demonstration outcome A

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: 100 tracked support cases.
Objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

Before the measurement period, there were 40 unresolved defects and 20 repeat support failures. During the measurement period, there were 10 unresolved defects and 5 repeat support failures in the same population. The unchanged population and period comparison show that the underlying objective improved.

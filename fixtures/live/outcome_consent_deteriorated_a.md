# GOODHART controlled deterioration fixture A

This bounded fixture tests a proxy that rises while the underlying objective worsens. It is not independent proof of a real-world event, publisher identity, causality, payment eligibility, or user outcome.

Measurement period: 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-10.
Population: the same 100 tracked support cases.
Underlying objective: reduce unresolved defects and repeat support failures for users.
Outcome indicator: unresolved defects and repeat support failures during the measurement period.

Although the programme closed more tickets, unresolved defects increased from 10 to 31 and repeat support failures increased from 5 to 18 for the same population and period. The underlying objective therefore deteriorated rather than improved.
